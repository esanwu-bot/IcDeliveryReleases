Technische Insights

Woran sollte beim Design mit ADS8568EVM-PDK gedacht werden?

  • Verify the Board Power Rails and Jumper Settings: This evaluation board is built around the onboard ADS8568. Confirm the rail selection, the current limit and the jumper positions against the board user guide before connecting any signal source; a wrong jumper is the most common cause of a board that appears dead.
  • Review the Thermal Relief and Grounding: At maximum output load it is the board, not the device, that sets the thermal limit. Provide adequate airflow and confirm the ground reference at the connector shields before trusting any measured performance figure.
