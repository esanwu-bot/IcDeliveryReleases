ADS1201U 16-Lead SOIC Delta-Sigma Modulator 130dB Texas Instruments

Der ADS1201 ist ein präziser Delta-Sigma-Modulator mit 130dB dynamischem Bereich, der von einer einzelnen +5V-Stromversorgung betrieben wird.

P

Überblick: ADS1201U-1/1K

ADS1201U-1/1K verfügt über ein völlig differentialer Eingang, eine Zwei-Draht-Schnittstelle und integrierte Kalibrierschalter. Mit dem richtigen digitalen Filter und Modulator-Takt werden 24-Bit-A/D-Conversion ohne fehlende Codes erreicht. Er ist für Hochauflösungsmessungen ausgelegt.

Zu den wichtigsten Funktionen gehören 130dB dynamic range, Fully differential input, Two-wire interface.

Es wird eingesetzt in Industrial process control, Instrumentation, Smart transmitters, Portable instruments.

ADS1201U-1/1K is supplied in the SOIC-16 package with 16 pins, an operating range of -40°C to +85°C, and 4 documented orderable options.

Technische Insights

Woran sollte beim Design mit ADS1201U-1/1K gedacht werden?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit Analog Input Voltage to AGND -0.3 V to AVDD +0.3 V; AVDD to DVDD to -0.3 V to 6 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to +85°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The SOIC-16 (16 pins), 3.9mm x 9.9mm defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

P

Pinbelegung

Die Pin-Belegung des SOIC-16 fasst 16 eindeutige Pin-Funktionen über 16 Anschlüsse.

  • 1 - AVDD: Analog Supply, +5V nominal
  • 2 - REFOUT: Internal Reference Voltage Output: +2.5V nominal
  • 3 - REFIN: Reference Voltage Input
  • 4 - NIC: Not Internally Connected
  • 5 - AINP: Noninverting Input
  • 6 - AINN: Inverting Input
  • 7 - AGND: Analog Ground
  • 8 - VBIAS: Bias Voltage Output, nominally +3.3V (with +2.5V reference)
  • 9 - BIASEN: Bias Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled)
  • 10 - GAIN/OFFSET: Gain/Offset Calibration Select Input
  • 11 - CAL: Calibration Control Input (HIGH = normal operation, LOW = gain or offset calibration)
  • 12 - DGND: Digital Ground
  • 13 - DVDD: Digital Supply, +5V nominal
  • 14 - MCLK: Modulator Clock Input. CMOS compatible.
  • 15 - MOUT: Modulator Output
  • 16 - REFEN: REF OUT Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled)

What distinguishes ADS1201U-1/1K from the other orderables?

ADS1201U-1/1K is the option documented on this page in the SOIC-16 package. Related order codes listed by the manufacturer include ADS1201U, ADS1201U/1K, ADS1201U/1KG4, ADS1201UG4; each differs in packing, temperature grade or package option.

Which electrical limits matter most for ADS1201U-1/1K?

The headline specifications are Absolute Input Voltage Range = 0 to +5 V; Differential Input Voltage Range = –5 to +5 V; Input Impedance = 250(4) TYP kΩ; Input Capacitance = 8 TYP pF. Always read them together with the stated test conditions.

What must be checked before laying out ADS1201U-1/1K?

Use the current SOIC-16 drawing and pin assignment (16 pins), keep the operating range (-40°C to +85°C) inside the enclosure budget, and verify the supply rails against the absolute maximum ratings.

What is ADS1201U-1/1K typically used for?
