Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with BOOSTXL-C2KLED?

  • Verify the Board Power Rails and Jumper Settings: This evaluation board is assembled from more than one device. Confirm the rail selection, the current limit and the jumper positions against the board user guide before connecting any signal source; a wrong jumper is the most common cause of a board that appears dead.
  • Review the Thermal Relief and Grounding: At maximum output load it is the board, not the device, that sets the thermal limit. Provide adequate airflow and confirm the ground reference at the connector shields before trusting any measured performance figure.
