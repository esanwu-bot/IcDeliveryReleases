NE555 Timer IC, 8-Pin, 5-15V, 200mA Output

The NE555 is a precision timing circuit capable of accurate time delays or oscillation.

P

NE555 Overview

NE555 operates from 5V to 15V supply, supports monostable and astable modes with adjustable duty cycle, and features TTL-compatible output that can sink or source up to 200mA. Ideal for pulse shaping, frequency division, and industrial control applications.

Key capabilities include timing from microseconds to hours; astable or monostable operation; adjustable duty cycle.

It is used in Pulse-shaping circuits, Missing-pulse detectors, Pulse-width modulators, Pulse-position modulators.

NE555 is supplied in the 8-Pin PDIP, SOIC, TSSOP, SO, CDIP, LCCC package with 8 pins, an operating range of -40°C to 105°C, and 17 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments document (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with NE555?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit VCC to 18 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to 105°C, storage -65°C to 150°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The 8-Pin PDIP, SOIC, TSSOP, SO, CDIP, LCCC (8 pins) defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

Package

NE555 uses the 8-Pin PDIP, SOIC, TSSOP, SO, CDIP, LCCC package with 8 pins.

The device is specified with an operating temperature range of -40°C to 105°C, and a storage range of -65°C to 150°C. Confirm the mechanical drawing in the current datasheet revision before release to production.

P

Pinout

The 8-Pin PDIP, SOIC, TSSOP, SO, CDIP, LCCC pin assignment groups 8 unique pin functions across 8 terminals.

  • 1 - GND: Ground
  • 2 - TRIG: Start of timing input. TRIG < ½ CONT sets output high and discharge open
  • 3 - OUT: High current timer output signal
  • 4 - RESET: Active low reset input forces output and discharge low
  • 5 - CONT: Controls comparator thresholds, Outputs 2/3 × VCC, allows bypass capacitor connection
  • 6 - THRES: End of timing input. THRES > CONT sets output low and discharge low
  • 7 - DISCH: Open collector output to discharge timing capacitor
  • 8 - VCC: Input supply voltage, 4.5V to 16V. SE555 maximum is 18V
