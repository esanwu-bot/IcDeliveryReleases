ADS42JB49 14-Bit 250-MSPS Dual-Channel ADC with JESD204B Interface

The ADS42JB49 is a dual-channel, 14-bit, 250-MSPS analog-to-digital converter with JESD204B serial interface.

P

ADS42JB49SEK Overview

ADS42JB49SEK features high linearity, differential full-scale input of 2 VPP and 2.5 VPP, aperture jitter of 85 fS rms, channel isolation of 100 dB, and power dissipation of 850 mW per channel. Designed for communication, radar, and test systems.

Key capabilities include dual-Channel ADCs; 14- and 16-Bit Resolution; maximum Clock Rate: 250 MSPS.

It is used in Communication and Cable Infrastructure, Multi-Carrier, Multimode Cellular Receivers, Radar and Smart Antenna Arrays, Broadband Wireless.

ADS42JB49SEK is supplied in the VQFN-64 package with 64 pins, an operating range of -40°C to +85°C, and 4 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments document (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with ADS42JB49SEK?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit AVDD3V to -0.3 to 3.6 V; AVDD to -0.3 to 2.1 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to +85°C, storage -65°C to 150°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The VQFN-64 (64 pins), 9 mm x 9 mm defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.
