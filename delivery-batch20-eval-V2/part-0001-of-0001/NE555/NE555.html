NE555 Precision Timer IC, 5V-15V, 8-Pin SOIC/PDIP

The NE555 is a precision timing circuit capable of producing accurate time delays or oscillation.

P

NE555 Overview

NE555 operates from 5V to 15V supply, supports monostable and astable modes with adjustable duty cycle, and features TTL-compatible output capable of sinking or sourcing up to 200mA. Suitable for pulse shaping, frequency division, and industrial control applications.

Key capabilities include timing from microseconds to hours; astable or monostable operation; adjustable duty cycle.

It is used in Pulse-shaping circuits, Missing-pulse detectors, Pulse-width modulators, Pulse-position modulators.

NE555 is supplied in the SOIC, PDIP, TSSOP package with 8 pins, an operating range of -40°C to 105°C, and 44 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments datasheet (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with NE555?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit VCC to 18 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to 105°C, storage -65°C to 150°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The SOIC, PDIP, TSSOP (8 pins) defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

P

Pinout

The SOIC, PDIP, TSSOP pin assignment groups 8 unique pin functions across 8 terminals.

  • 1 - GND: Ground
  • 2 - TRIG: Start of timing input. TRIG < ½ CONT sets output high and discharge open
  • 3 - OUT: High current timer output signal
  • 4 - RESET: Active low reset input forces output and discharge low
  • 5 - CONT: Controls comparator thresholds, Outputs 2/3 × VCC, allows bypass capacitor connection
  • 6 - THRES: End of timing input. THRES > CONT sets output low and discharge low
  • 7 - DISCH: Open collector output to discharge timing capacitor
  • 8 - VCC: Input supply voltage, 4.5V to 16V. SE555 maximum is 18V

Frequently Asked Questions

What distinguishes NE555 from the other orderables?

NE555 is the option documented on this page in the SOIC, PDIP, TSSOP package. Related order codes listed by the manufacturer include JM38510/10901BPA, JM38510/10901BPA.A, NA555D, NA555DR; each differs in packing, temperature grade or package option.

Which electrical limits matter most for NE555?

The headline specifications are THRES voltage level = 8.8 to 11.2 V, THRES voltage level = 9.4 to 10.6 V, THRES voltage level = 2.4 to 4.2 V, and THRES voltage level = 2.7 to 4 V. Always read them together with the stated test conditions.

What must be checked before laying out NE555?

Use the current SOIC, PDIP, TSSOP drawing and pin assignment (8 pins), keep the operating range (-40°C to 105°C) inside the enclosure budget, and verify the supply rails against the absolute maximum ratings.

What is NE555 typically used for?

Typical applications include Pulse-shaping circuits, Missing-pulse detectors, Pulse-width modulators, and Pulse-position modulators.
