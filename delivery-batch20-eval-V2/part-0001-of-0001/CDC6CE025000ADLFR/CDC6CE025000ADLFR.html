CDC6CE025000ADLFR Low Power LVCMOS BAW Oscillator 25MHz

Texas Instruments' CDC6CE025000ADLFR is a low power, LVCMOS output BAW oscillator with frequency range from 250kHz to 200MHz.

P

CDC6CE025000ADLFR Overview

CDC6CE025000ADLFR features ±25ppm total frequency stability over -40°C to 105°C, supply voltage from 1.8V to 3.3V, and typical current consumption of 4.32mA at 25MHz and 1.8V. Designed for high-performance clocking in industrial, telecom, data, and personal electronics applications.

Key capabilities include LVCMOS output oscillator supporting frequency range from 250kHz to 200MHz; ±25ppm total frequency stability inclusive of all factors and including 10 years aging; supply voltage supports 1.8V to 3.3V ±10%.

It is used in Crystal oscillator replacement, Data centers, Server, Storage, Ethernet, SAS, SATA, USB, WIFI, Wireless Communication.

CDC6CE025000ADLFR is supplied in the VSON (DLF) package with 4 pins, an operating range of -40°C to 105°C, and 1 documented orderable option.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments datasheet (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with CDC6CE025000ADLFR?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit VDD to -0.3 to 3.63 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to 105°C, storage -65°C to 150°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The VSON (DLF) (4 pins), 2.50mm × 2.00mm defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.
