ADS1201U-1/1K Delta-Sigma Modulators

The ADS1201 is a precision, 130dB dynamic range, delta-sigma modulator operating from a single +5V supply.

P

ADS1201U-1/1K Overview

ADS1201U-1/1K features fully differential inputs, a two-wire interface, and on-chip calibration switches. With an appropriate digital filter and modulator rate, it achieves 24-bit A/D conversion without missing codes. It is suitable for high-resolution measurement applications such as industrial process control, weigh scales, and smart transmitters. Available in a 16-lead SOIC package.

Key capabilities include 130dB dynamic range; fully differential input; two-wire interface.

It is used in Industrial process control, Instrumentation, Smart transmitters, Portable instruments.

ADS1201U-1/1K is supplied in the SOIC-16 package with 16 pins, an operating range of -40°C to +85°C, and 4 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments datasheet (source PDF).

What should be checked when designing with ADS1201U-1/1K?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit Analog Input Voltage to -0.3 to 6 V; AVDD to DVDD to -0.3 to 6 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.

  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The SOIC-16 (16 pins), 3.9mm x 9.9mm defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

Pinout

The SOIC-16 pin assignment groups 16 unique pin functions across 16 terminals.

  • 1 - AVDD: Analog Supply, +5V nominal
  • 2 - REFOUT: Internal Reference Voltage Output: +2.5V nominal
  • 3 - REFIN: Reference Voltage Input
  • 4 - NIC: Not Internally Connected
  • 5 - AINP: Noninverting Input
  • 6 - AINN: Inverting Input
  • 7 - AGND: Analog Ground
  • 8 - VBIAS: Bias Voltage Output, nominally +3.3V (with +2.5V reference)
  • 9 - BIASEN: Bias Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled)
  • 10 - GAIN/OFFSET: Gain/Offset Calibration Select Input
  • 11 - CAL: Calibration Control Input (HIGH = normal operation, LOW = gain or offset calibration)
  • 12 - DGND: Digital Ground
  • 13 - DVDD: Digital Supply, +5V nominal
  • 14 - MCLK: Modulator Clock Input. CMOS compatible.
  • 15 - MOUT: Modulator Output
  • 16 - REFEN: REF OUT Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled)
