LM358 Dual Op Amp, 3V-36V, 1.2MHz Bandwidth

The LM358 is a dual operational amplifier with a wide supply range of 3V to 36V, low quiescent current of 300µA per channel, and unity-gain bandwidth of 1.2MHz.

P

LM358 Overview

LM358 features input offset voltage up to 3mV (max), common-mode input range including ground, and high ESD tolerance (2kV HBM). Suitable for cost-sensitive applications in power supplies, motor control, and industrial systems.

Key capabilities include wide supply range of 3V to 36V; quiescent current: 300µA/ch; unity-gain bandwidth of 1.2MHz.

It is used in Merchant network and server power supply units, Multi-function printers, Power supplies and mobile chargers, Motor control: AC induction, brushed DC, brushless DC, high-voltage, low-voltage, permanent magnet, and stepper motor.

LM358 is supplied in the SOIC (D) package with 8 pins, an operating range of -40°C to 85°C, and 24 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments datasheet (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with LM358?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit Supply voltage to ±20 or 40 V; Differential input voltage to -32 to 32 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to 85°C, storage -65°C to 150°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The SOIC (D) (8 pins), 4.9mm x 6mm defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

Pinout

The SOIC (D) pin assignment groups 8 unique pin functions across 8 terminals.

  • 1 - OUT1: Output
  • 2 - IN1–: Negative input
  • 3 - IN1+: Positive input
  • 4 - V–: Negative (lowest) supply or ground (for single- supply operation) NC 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 16, 18, 19 — — No internal connection
  • 5 - IN2+: Positive input
  • 6 - IN2–: Negative input
  • 7 - OUT2: Output
  • 8 - V+: Positive (highest) supply
