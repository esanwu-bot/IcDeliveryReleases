ADS1201U-1/1K Delta-Sigma Modulator, 130dB Dynamic Range, SOIC-16

The ADS1201 is a precision, 130dB dynamic range delta-sigma modulator operating from a single +5V supply.

P

ADS1201U-1/1K Overview

ADS1201U-1/1K features fully differential inputs, a two-wire interface, internal/external reference support, and on-chip calibration switches. Ideal for high-resolution measurement applications such as smart transmitters, industrial process control, weigh scales, chromatography, and portable instrumentation.

Key capabilities include 130dB DYNAMIC RANGE; FULLY DIFFERENTIAL INPUT; TWO-WIRE INTERFACE.

It is used in INDUSTRIAL PROCESS CONTROL, INSTRUMENTATION, SMART TRANSMITTERS, PORTABLE INSTRUMENTS.

ADS1201U-1/1K is supplied in the SOIC package with 16 pins, an operating range of -40°C to +85°C, and 4 documented orderable options.

The values on this page come from the Texas Instruments datasheet (source PDF).

Technical Insight

What should be checked when designing with ADS1201U-1/1K?

  • Respect the Absolute Maximum Rails: Absolute maximum ratings limit Analog Input: Voltage to AGND –0.3V to AVDD +0.3V; AVDD to DVDD to -0.3 to 6 V. Design the supply sequence and transients inside these limits before selecting surrounding components.
  • Budget the Thermal Headroom: Operating temperature is -40°C to +85°C. Derating, self-heating and airflow must be verified at the worst-case ambient of the enclosure.
  • Lock the Package and Pinout Early: The SOIC (16 pins) defines the footprint, thermal path and pin assignment; confirm it against the current revision before layout.

P

Pinout

The SOIC pin assignment groups 16 unique pin functions across 16 terminals.

  • 1 - AVDD: Analog Supply, +5V nominal.
  • 2 - REFOUT: Internal Reference Voltage Output: +2.5V nominal.
  • 3 - REFIN: Reference Voltage Input.
  • 4 - NIC: Not Internally Connected.
  • 5 - AINP: Noninverting Input.
  • 6 - AINN: Inverting Input.
  • 7 - AGND: Analog Ground.
  • 8 - VBIAS: Bias Voltage Output, nominally +3.3V (with +2.5V reference).
  • 9 - BIASEN: Bias Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled).
  • 10 - GAIN/OFFSET: Gain/Offset Calibration Select Input (with CAL LOW; HIGH = gain configuration, LOW = offset configuration).
  • 11 - CAL: Calibration Control Input (HIGH = normal operation, LOW = gain or offset calibration configuration).
  • 12 - DGND: Digital Ground.
  • 13 - DVDD: Digital Supply, +5V nominal.
  • 14 - MCLK: Modulator Clock Input. CMOS compatible.
  • 15 - MOUT: Modulator Output.
  • 16 - REFEN: REFOUT Voltage Enable Input (HIGH = enabled, LOW = disabled).
